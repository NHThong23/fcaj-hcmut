### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at the FCAJ office (26th floor, Bitexco Tower) is very friendly and welcoming. The workspace is comfortable with flexible hours — I can proactively manage my schedule without feeling constrained. The office is well-equipped with full amenities, air-conditioned throughout the day, helping me stay focused despite the scorching heat of Saigon. Right from day one, I received a warm welcome and dedicated support from everyone in the team, which helped me quickly settle in and catch up with the workflow.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor is incredibly dedicated — whenever I faced difficulties, he patiently explained things in detail until I truly understood, while always encouraging me to proactively ask questions rather than staying silent. On the admin side, the team provided swift support with paperwork, documentation, and environment setup, ensuring I was never held back by administrative hurdles. What I appreciate most is that the mentor never simply handed me the answer — he always let me explore, make mistakes, and draw my own conclusions. That's a truly effective and memorable way to learn.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As a Computer Science student, I place a strong emphasis on hands-on practice — theory from lectures only truly has value when applied to real-world problems. Throughout the internship, I was assigned tasks that were appropriate for my skill level, closely aligned with what I learned in school, yet still challenging enough to push me. More than that, I had the opportunity to work with entirely new technologies and tools I had never encountered before — from cloud computing and CI/CD to various AWS services. This intersection of foundational knowledge and practical technology helped me both reinforce my expertise and significantly expand my skillset.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship was a major boost for my skill development. Beyond programming expertise, I learned to use project management tools like Jira and Git, practiced effective team collaboration through daily standups, and — most importantly — gradually built confidence in professional communication, from writing emails and presenting ideas to the team to reporting progress to my mentor. Above all, the mentor didn't just teach technical skills; he also shared real-world lessons from his own industry experience, giving me a much clearer vision of the career path ahead.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
What impressed me most about FCAJ is the work culture — where professionalism and a "one-team" spirit coexist naturally. Everyone in the team, regardless of seniority, treats one another with respect and goodwill. The atmosphere is focused but never tense; casual conversations and shared lunches make every day at the office light and something to look forward to. Especially when urgent deadlines hit, no one stands on the sidelines — everyone rolls up their sleeves and helps out, no matter their rank or role. Thanks to that environment, even as an intern, I never once felt out of place — I truly felt like part of the team.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible working hours when needed. In addition, having access to internal training sessions is a significant plus.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?
  The thing I'm most satisfied with is that FCAJ is not just an internship — it's a community. I got to meet, connect with, and learn from friends from many different universities across Vietnam. That diversity of perspectives and the spirit of open sharing made my internship experience truly special.

- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?
+ Overall, the company has created excellent conditions for interns. However, I have a couple of small suggestions: setting up a free drinking water area so people can refill without having to remember to buy water. Additionally, opening the office on more days each week (instead of just 2–3 sessions) would give interns more time to work on-site, make better use of the facilities, and foster more interaction with the team.

- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?
+ Absolutely yes. FCAJ is an ideal environment for students who want real-world exposure in tech — especially those interested in cloud computing and the AWS ecosystem. Here, you don't just learn from experienced mentors; you also connect with a vibrant student community, attend free monthly workshops, and — most importantly — are empowered to experiment, make mistakes, and grow. I believe anyone who goes through this internship will come out a better version of themselves.

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?
+ Beyond the suggestions mentioned above, I think the program would be even better with a short onboarding session on the first day to help interns understand the roadmap, tasks, and support channels. Additionally, organizing regular check-ins (e.g., every 2 weeks) between mentors and interns would help track progress more closely and resolve challenges before they become major roadblocks.

- Would you like to continue this program in the future?
+ What I gained from FCAJ — knowledge, skills, connections, and confidence — far exceeded my initial expectations. If given the opportunity, I would love to return in a higher role, perhaps as a mentor for the next generation of interns, to keep learning while giving back to the community that helped me so much.
